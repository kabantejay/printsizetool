Large-format printing from a regular home printer

This tool creates a multi-page or “tiled” PDF. Your image is scaled to an exact physical width and height, divided into printable sections, and placed on standard sheets such as US Letter, A4, Legal, Tabloid or A3.

How to print and assemble your PDF

  1. Upload your image and enter its desired finished size.
  2. Choose the paper loaded in your printer.
  3. Download the PDF and print at 100% / Actual size. Do not use “Fit to page”.
  4. Trim one side of each overlap, align the matching marks, then tape the sheets together.

Frequently asked questions

Does the image get uploaded?

No. Image processing and PDF generation happen locally in your browser.

How do I make a 40 × 40 cm pattern?

Select centimeters, enter 40 for width and 40 for height, choose your paper size, then download and print the PDF at Actual size or 100% scale.

Why is overlap useful?

Overlap repeats a narrow strip of the design on neighboring sheets. It makes alignment easier and leaves room for trimming and taping.

Should I mirror a craft pattern?

Mirror the image when your transfer method reverses the design, such as tracing from the back or using certain transfer papers. Leave it off when orientation does not matter.

What resolution do I need?

Line art often works well around 150 DPI at final size. Detailed artwork may benefit from 200-300 DPI. The tool displays the effective DPI of your uploaded file.
